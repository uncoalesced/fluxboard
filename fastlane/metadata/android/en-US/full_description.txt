FluxBoard is an open-source Android keyboard with a full sticker studio and an offline, self-learning typing engine. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no Google Play Services dependency, and no account.

Keyboard and typing
- Local Bloom autocorrect and glide decoding backed by compiled dictionaries and a local bigram model.
- BloomType adaptive touch and glide geometry learning, kept on your device with quick revert recovery when you backspace a correction.
- Custom key layouts and keyboard themes with background images, opacity controls, and live previews.
- Local clipboard history that skips password fields, incognito fields, private mode, and Android-flagged sensitive content.

Stickers and media
- Import photos into your sticker library, crop framing manually, erase backgrounds with a brush eraser and undo, and add styled text overlays.
- Trim imported videos into animated GIF or WebP stickers directly on your phone.
- Organize stickers into categories, mark favorites, and insert them from the keyboard into any app that accepts images.

Device migration
- Move your sticker library, categories, favorites, custom layouts, themes, and optional clipboard history to a new phone over your local Wi-Fi network by scanning a QR pairing code, with no cloud server in the middle.

Typing, prediction learning, and sticker editing never leave your device. Network access is used only during a user-initiated local Wi-Fi transfer.
