# Authors

FluxBoard is written by two people.

**uncoalesced** — the keyboard and most of what sits under it. The IME itself,
prediction and autocorrect, glide typing, theming and layouts, the clipboard,
the data layer, device pairing and transfer, and the sticker pipeline.

**ZapCannonYT** — design tokens, sticker organisation, gallery import, the video
trim UI, and the research behind the segmentation and platform-compatibility
decisions. Documentation from here on.

Copyright 2026 uncoalesced and ZapCannonYT, under the [MIT licence](../LICENSE).

## Credit for contributions

Anyone who contributes keeps their own commit authorship, which is the record that
matters. This file lists the two people responsible for the project as a whole,
not everyone who has ever helped; for that, the contributors graph is accurate and
does not need maintaining by hand.

## A note on tooling

Parts of this project were written with the help of AI coding tools, which is
worth saying plainly rather than leaving people to guess from the commit style.
Every line was reviewed before it was committed, and the two names above are
accountable for all of it.

Earlier commits in this repository carried a `Co-Authored-By` trailer crediting an
AI model. It was added automatically by tooling rather than chosen, it made a
model look like a third member of the project, and it has been removed from the
history. Authorship here is human.
